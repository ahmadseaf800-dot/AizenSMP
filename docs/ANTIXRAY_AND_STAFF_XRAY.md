# AizenSMP - Anti-Xray & Staff X-Ray

## Player protection
- Anti-Xray is enabled at the server/world level.
- Hidden ores and valuable blocks must not be exposed through normal client X-Ray/resource-pack tricks.
- Use Purpur/Paper Anti-Xray or an equivalent server-side configuration.
- Protection must cover overworld ores and Nether valuables.
- Test with spectator/admin tools before production.

## Staff X-Ray
- Staff X-Ray is an admin/staff-only feature.
- It must use a separate permission and never be granted to normal players.
- Every toggle/use should be auditable.
- Staff X-Ray must not modify or duplicate blocks/items.
- Recommended permission: `aizensmp.staff.xray`.
