# AizenSMP - Ready Spawn Specification

## Style
Modern SMP spawn, medium size, clean paths, central arrival platform, easy navigation and low visual clutter.

## Required areas
1. Central Spawn
2. Shop
3. Auction House information
4. Crates + Keys
5. Kits
6. RTP
7. Rules
8. Leaderboards
9. PvP/Combat information
10. Safe exits toward the survival world

## Build rules
- Keep the actual spawn point unobstructed.
- No combat at the central spawn.
- Use signs/NPCs only where supported by the chosen plugin stack.
- Keep paths wide enough for players.
- Avoid excessive entities to reduce lag.
- The final spawn can be delivered as a WorldEdit/FAWE schematic or as a separate world.

## Installation
Paste/import the spawn, set the exact center location, then run:
 /setspawn
